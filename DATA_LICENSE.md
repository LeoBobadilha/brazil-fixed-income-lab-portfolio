# Data licence and attribution

The **code** in this repository is MIT-licensed (see `LICENSE`).

The **datasets** in `web/public/data/` are derived databases built from:

- Tesouro Nacional – Tesouro Transparente: *Taxas dos Títulos Ofertados pelo Tesouro Direto* and *Estoque da Dívida Pública Federal* (Open Database License, ODbL 1.0)
- Banco Central do Brasil – SGS time series and Focus market expectations (ODbL 1.0)
- IBGE – IPCA number index, SIDRA table 1737 (public data, attribution required)

As required by the ODbL, the derived datasets are made available under the
**Open Database License (ODbL) 1.0**: https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/

and IBGE." The original data are provided by these institutions, which do not endorse this project.

are the project's own calculations.
